Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

EvenBetterRYM is a browser extension that adds quality-of-life improvements to RateYourMusic.com.

Summary

EvenBetterRYM does not collect, store, or transmit any user data.

Details

The extension runs entirely on your device. It does not operate any servers, does not use analytics or tracking of any kind, and does not send your browsing activity, account information, or any other personal data anywhere.

Any requests the extension makes to third-party services (such as streaming platforms, for linking release pages to matching tracks/albums) are made directly from your browser to that service, using only the information needed to perform the lookup you initiated. EvenBetterRYM itself does not see, log, or store the results of those requests.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue on the GitHub repository.
